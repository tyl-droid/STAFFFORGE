
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making StaffForge a respectful, safe, and welcoming project for everyone.

All contributors, users, and community members are expected to act with professionalism, kindness, and integrity.

---

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful
* Helping others
* Giving constructive feedback
* Respecting privacy
* Reporting issues responsibly
* Being patient with beginners
* Keeping discussions on-topic

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment
* Hate speech
* Threats or intimidation
* Doxxing or sharing private information
* Scamming
* Impersonation
* Malicious code submissions
* Spam
* Abusive language
* Attempts to sell fake or unofficial StaffForge builds

---

## Community Safety

StaffForge is connected to a wider community safety mission through Community Shield Network and BLACKGRID.

Users should avoid:

* Exploiting others
* Encouraging dishonesty in staff applications
* Using StaffForge to misrepresent themselves
* Distributing unsafe modified builds

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

* Issue or comment removal
* Pull request rejection
* Repository blocking
* Scam reports being documented
* Reporting to relevant platforms when necessary

---

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to:

[CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com](mailto:CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com)

Please include:

* What happened
* Screenshots or links
* Usernames involved
* Any relevant context

Reports will be reviewed as fairly as possible.
