# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is StaffForge free?

Yes. StaffForge is completely free.

---

## Does StaffForge submit applications automatically?

No. StaffForge never submits applications automatically.

All submissions require manual review and action by the user.

---

## Can the developers see my data?

No. By default, your data is stored locally on your device.

---

## Is cloud sync required?

No. Cloud sync is optional.

---

## Is StaffForge affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft?

No. StaffForge is independent and not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

---

## Can I use StaffForge for any Minecraft server?

Yes. StaffForge is designed to work with applications for any Minecraft server.

---

## Can I donate?

Yes. Donations are optional and help support development.

Official Ko-fi:

[https://ko-fi.com/communityshieldofficial](https://ko-fi.com/communityshieldofficial)

---

## Does donating unlock premium features?

No. StaffForge remains free.

Donations do not unlock premium features or special access.

---

## What should I do if someone sells me StaffForge?

Do not pay.

Report it to:

[CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com](mailto:CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com)

If you believe you were scammed, report it to your payment provider and local cybercrime authority.
