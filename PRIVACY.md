This document explains where to get support, how to report problems, and how to request help safely.

## Before Asking for Help

Please check:

1. The README
2. The FAQ
3. Existing GitHub Issues
4. Your terminal error messages
5. Whether dependencies are installed

---

## What to Include

When asking for support, include:

* What you were trying to do
* What happened
* Error messages
* Screenshots
* Operating system version
* Node.js version
* Steps you already tried

---

## Example Support Request

```text
I tried running npm run desktop, but Electron did not open.
I am using Windows 11.
Node version: 24.x
Error message: ...
I already tried npm install.
```

---

## Scam or Fake Version Reports

If someone tries to sell StaffForge or sends you a suspicious build, contact:

[CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com](mailto:CommunityShieldOfficial@gmail.com)

Include evidence such as screenshots, usernames, download links, or payment requests.

---

## What Support Cannot Do

We cannot:

* Guarantee acceptance into any Minecraft staff team
* Submit applications for you
* Access your local StaffForge data
* Recover data stored only on your device
* Provide legal or financial advice

# Privacy Policy

StaffForge is designed as a privacy-first, local-first productivity tool.

This document explains how StaffForge handles user data.

---

## Local-First by Default

By default, StaffForge stores data locally on your own device.

This may include:

* Profiles
* Application answers
* Notes
* Analytics
* Settings
* Interview preparation notes
* Email parsing results
* Calendar reminder data

---

## We Do Not Have Access to Your Local Data

Community Shield Network, BLACKGRID, and the developer do not have access to your local StaffForge data.

We cannot:

* View your applications
* Read your saved answers
* Access your database
* Monitor your emails
* Remotely access your device
* Retrieve your local files

---

## Optional Cloud Sync

StaffForge may include optional cloud sync features using services such as Supabase.

Cloud sync is optional.

If enabled:

* You control the account and configuration
* Data is sent only to services you configure
* You can disable sync
* You can delete synced data from your chosen service

---

## Gmail and Calendar Integrations

Google integrations are optional.

If configured, they may process:

* Application update emails
* Interview invitation emails
* Calendar reminder details

These integrations should only operate with user consent and configured credentials.

---

## No Hidden Telemetry

StaffForge does not include hidden analytics, spyware, or invasive tracking.

Any future analytics or telemetry must be:

* Opt-in
* Documented
* Easy to disable
* Clearly explained

---

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for:

* Protecting their device
* Keeping backups
* Managing cloud credentials
* Reviewing generated answers
* Submitting truthful applications
